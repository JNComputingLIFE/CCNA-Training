lab 2 - subnets from subnets

The real first step of the problem:
binary to find subnets of subnets..
of note is first subnet:
192.168.1.64/28
192.168.1.80/28
192.168.1.96/28
192.168.1.112/28

the last subnet will be used for a link (don't forget /30 is router router interface connections). 

Therefore, what is needed is to split 2 host per subnet again so use
2 ^ n - 2 again (since you want /30 you know this gives 2 needed):

255.255.255.11200 00, 01, 10, 11 AKA 0 (.112/30), .116/30, .120/30, .124/30

This is good to use if you wish to split a router to router connection safely.
