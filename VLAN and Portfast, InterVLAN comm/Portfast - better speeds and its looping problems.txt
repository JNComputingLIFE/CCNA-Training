PORTFAST - FAST BUT LOOPS

- cisco proprietary

- "bypasses listening and learning" 

- if connected to a layer 2 switch may cause loops


WHAT PORTFAST SOLVES
if it takes some time to get an ip address from dhcp (if ports transition into forwarding then listening state they may get stuck later at learning state)
- ironically it can prevent or make loops


normally spanning tree takes 30seconds to converge and forward packets..
- so portfast prevents this and is more helpful for modern networks

steps:
1.give a hostname with hostname command.
2. int range gX/X/X - Y (where X-Y is connected devices)
3. spanning-tree portfast  
- loops can happen. That's why it only available in non-trunking mode.

it will move states immediately to forwarding ((AKA forwarding packets..))
