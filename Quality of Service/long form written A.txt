What is the issue?

Policy maps match any by default in inter-routing communication. 
Routers communicate in separate protocols (routing ones like ospf).
With ICMP, you can ping and see the correct applied QoS.
- in QoS, you must match in/out (of routers in this lab) traffic correctly, otherwise it will be seen as default traffic.
